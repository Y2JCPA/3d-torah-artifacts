# Tefillin Shel Yad — v0.2.0-alpha.2 research preview

One hollow chamber, one continuous rolled parchment with four identified passages, square titura, twelve sinew stitch locations, parchment wrapping, animal hair and a real strap tunnel. No chamber grooves or side shins. Nine selectable components share the temporary highlighting and source-classified lessons used by Shel Rosh.

The compact yud fold study and loosened oval loop use the visible illustrations in the Shluchim Office / Rabbi DovBer Pinson guide. The housing is bare leather, without the protective casing illustrated elsewhere in that guide; no casing hole is copied into the bayit. One continuous ribbon joins the folded return, tunnel, loop and longer strap. This geometric continuity does not establish a correct knot-tying route. Hidden crossings, proportions and community-specific details remain schematic.

Serve the collection root with `python3 -m http.server 4173`, then choose Shel Yad in the gallery. “All artifacts” returns to the collection. Dependencies are local vendor assets under the Shel Rosh folder and shared lesson/selection modules. No build step is needed.

The reference illustrations are not bundled. This is not a manufacturing guide, wearing instruction, verified tying sequence or kosher certification. See QA.md and SOURCES.md.
