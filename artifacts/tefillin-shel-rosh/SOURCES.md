## Knot reference review for 0.8.0-alpha.1

The user's private outer-face photograph was actually viewed after checksum verification. It supports the horizontal collar, descending folded section, narrow right-edge overlap, and visible strap branches. It does not reveal the underside or establish a crossing sequence. The photograph is not distributed.

The [Shluchim Office illustrated guide](https://www.shluchim.org/content/img/tefillinguide.pdf), printed page 7, supports the low, wide passage and visible folded knot form, not a complete hidden tying route. [Shulchan Arukh HaRav 32:79](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/3284478/jewish/Shulchan-Aruch-Chapter-32-Laws-Relating-to-Writing-of-Tefillin.htm) describes the outward orientation of the dalet. The previously listed Bais Yisroel PDF was unavailable and was not used as route evidence. No complete crossing table has been verified; the square/double-dalet choice is disabled.
