## Knot model limitations

The single-dalet study shows a horizontal collar, descending fold and narrow overlap. Concealed crossings are schematic; this is not a knot-tying guide.

The [Shluchim Office illustrated guide](https://www.shluchim.org/content/img/tefillinguide.pdf), printed page 7, supports the low, wide passage and visible folded knot form, not a complete hidden tying route. [Shulchan Arukh HaRav 32:79](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/3284478/jewish/Shulchan-Aruch-Chapter-32-Laws-Relating-to-Writing-of-Tefillin.htm) describes the outward orientation of the dalet. The previously listed Bais Yisroel PDF was unavailable and was not used as route evidence. No complete crossing table has been verified; the square/double-dalet choice was disabled in v0.8. The square form has the limitations described below.

## Square/double-dalet model limitations (v0.9)

The square/double-dalet study shows a four-panel outer form with diagonal branches. Its hidden crossings and tying sequence remain unverified. No claim of historical priority is made.
