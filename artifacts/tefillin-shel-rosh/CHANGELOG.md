# Changelog

Semantic versioning is used. Versions below 1.0 are research previews, not declarations of completeness or craft approval.

## 0.8.0-alpha.1 — 2026-10-29

Review candidate focused on straps and direct model interaction. Not a verified knot-tying reconstruction.

- Rebuilt the straps as one ribbon with two free ends: tail, knot zone, head loop, transverse maabarta passage, second knot zone, other tail. Semantic mesh partitions share the same boundary vertices.
- Inspected a checksum-verified private crop of the user's knot photograph. Replaced the loose single-dalet silhouette with a broad outer collar, lower folded return and narrow right-edge overlap. The unseen reverse route remains explicitly schematic.
- Withheld the inaccurate square/double-dalet study pending a reliable reference, and moved the mobile model below camera controls.
- Added close-up camera targets for the knot and real open-ended strap tunnel.
- Filtered picking by ancestor visibility and cut planes. Persistent drag tracking and multi-contact guards prevent orbit/pinch gestures from selecting parts.
- Hide numbered parchment markers when occluded or viewed from the rear; wait for actual visibility in browser checks.
- Added real mouse/touch mesh tests for every major topic across all three modes, including hidden interiors, repeated knot/order changes and gesture guards.
- Added thin connected shin relief, rolled-sheet parchment geometry and leather surface refinements. These remain illustrative.
- Removed the inaccessible knot-comparison PDF link in favor of an accessible illustrated guide.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-10-29

Rebuilt the standalone educational model after review of v0.6.0 source and browser reproduction of its core geometry failures.

- Replaced overlapping chamber groups with four fixed, hollow compartments in a single square-plan housing.
- Replaced disappearing-box reveal logic with explicit opaque cutaway geometry and a reversible exploded diagram of the joined housing, upright scrolls, and base.
- Oriented scrolls vertically and represented insertion from beneath. Removed speculative folded-square geometry and pseudo-sacred lettering.
- Added fine hair bindings, parchment wrappers, rolled edges and procedural fibers, distinct from perimeter sinew stitching.
- Added integral-looking raised leather shin relief on the correct wearer sides, an open-ended rear passage, continuous strap routing and clearly labeled schematic knot variants.
- Added twelve selectable topics with primary sources, verified Rashi/Rabbeinu Tam identification and an explicit viewing perspective.
- Added camera presets, zoom buttons, numbered scroll callouts, reduced-motion support, mobile layout, and comprehensive reset.
- Bundled Three.js and add-ons for offline local use.
- Corrected defects found by rendering screenshots: a ribbon sampling runtime error, excessive glare, bowed grooves, camera travel through the model, mobile framing, and missing-glyph link decorations.
- Added browser interaction checks, screenshot evidence, source notes, and honest review limitations.
