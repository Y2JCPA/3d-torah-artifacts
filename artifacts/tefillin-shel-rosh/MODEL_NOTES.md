# Geometry and viewing conventions

## Coordinates

Y is up. The front of the tefillin is positive Z; the maabarta and rear knot are negative Z. Viewed from the front, screen left corresponds to negative X and the wearer’s right. Thus:

| Location | World X | Shin |
|---|---:|---|
| Wearer’s right | negative | three heads |
| Wearer’s left | positive | four heads |

The housing spans three illustrative units in X and Z. Its height is not treated as a halakhically mandated dimension. Four chambers are spaced at X = −1.125, −0.375, +0.375, +1.125. Their positions do not move sideways during reveal animations. The leather skirts join the chamber geometry.

## Passage arrangement

The UI numbers chambers from the **front-facing viewer’s left to right**, which is the wearer’s right to left.

| Chamber | Rashi | Rabbeinu Tam |
|---|---|---|
| 1 / wearer’s right | Vehaya im shamoa | Shema |
| 2 | Shema | Vehaya im shamoa |
| 3 | Vehaya ki yeviakha | Vehaya ki yeviakha |
| 4 / wearer’s left | Kadesh | Kadesh |

The scroll surfaces deliberately contain no readable passage text. Their identity is shown through the numbered diagram and selected-component description. Changing the order changes those identities, not the construction geometry.

## Display modes

- **Assembled:** opaque, closed housing; scrolls physically inside it.
- **Exploded:** housing moves upward as one unit; upright scrolls and the base are separated vertically for study. The model is a diagram, not an instruction to detach the actual construction.
- **Cutaway:** one front clipping plane removes leather only. Thin section faces show where the internal chamber walls were cut. The parchment is revealed by that section, not pushed through the side wall.

No mode uses transparency to make chamber boxes disappear. Highlight materials retain their clipping state. Interrupted animations always derive from one current mode and converge to its target. Camera transitions interpolate around the subject instead of crossing through it.

## Materials and craft limits

The texture is generated locally. It suggests black painted leather and parchment fibers but does not reproduce a measured artifact. Raised shin silhouettes are beveled teaching approximations. Stitch placement illustrates twelve stitches; it is not a ruling that this is the only possible count. Hair bindings and sinew sewing are separate geometry and explanation topics.

The rear tunnel is open at both ends along X. The strap passes through it, curves around the laid-out head loop, and continues through the single-dalet study into two tails. Exact knot interlacing has not been reconstructed or validated. No community is assigned one universal knot form.

The hair–parchment–hair arrangement follows the Rema discussion. Inner hair is modeled beneath the wrapper and is occluded in the normal teaching view. No canonical dimensions or folding pattern are asserted.
